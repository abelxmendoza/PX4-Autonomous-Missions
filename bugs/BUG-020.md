BUG-020 (open -- known gap, documented not fixed)

Scenario:
Fault injection, `frozen_sensor` on the IMU: the IMU keeps delivering messages
with advancing timestamps but repeats its first sample for 5 s.

Requirement violated:
Fault-injection expectation for `frozen_sensor` (detect a stuck sensor, bounded
error growth).

Symptom (offline rig, seed 1):
- With VO healthy (`sensor_faults.yaml`): no detection, but also no measurable
  harm (error growth +0.003 m) -- the 5 Hz VO velocity updates mask it.
- With VO unavailable (`imu_faults_during_vo_outage.yaml`): +32.1 m peak
  horizontal error above baseline over the window, never flagged.

Root cause:
There is no IMU stuck-sensor detector. `ekf_fusion_node` judges IMU health only
by message age, and a frozen IMU still produces fresh messages.

Why it is not fixed yet:
The VO-side equivalent (BUG-019) was safe to add because five recorded flights
let me measure its false-positive rate. No raw IMU recording is archived, so an
exact-repeat rule for SensorCombined cannot yet be shown not to fire on a
healthy (possibly noise-free in SITL) IMU. Shipping an untested detector in the
control path would be worse than documenting the gap.

Next step:
Record `SensorCombined` from a few SITL flights (and hover on hardware if
available), measure how often all six channels repeat bit-for-bit, then add the
rule with that evidence. The two affected scenarios carry `known_gap: BUG-020`;
`test_the_only_known_gaps_are_the_documented_ones` pins them, and a stale marker
fails CI once the fault starts passing.
