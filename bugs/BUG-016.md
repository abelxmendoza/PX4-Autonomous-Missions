BUG-016 (re-diagnosed; detection added, root causes remain open)

Scenario:
Reactive "waypoints" mission through the obstacle field, climb/sidestep
avoidance. First seen as: altitude jumped from ~2-3 m to 11.6 m in ~3 s right
as the vehicle left the GPS-denied zone, breaching the 12 m ceiling.

Requirement violated:
REQ-GEOFENCE-01 caught the altitude breach, but the breach was a consequence.
The underlying failure is loss of control after a collision, which no
requirement covered. Two now do: REQ-ATT-01 (no loss of control) and
REQ-CLEARANCE-02 (airframe never touches a mapped obstacle).

Symptom:
Altitude "spike", then geofence FAILSAFE.

What it actually is (the original attribution to GPS-zone exit was wrong):
Across the 68 recorded flight logs that carry attitude data, 29 contain a
>60 degree roll or pitch while airborne. In those:
- reactive avoidance was active at onset in 27 of 29;
- only 5 of 29 began inside the GPS-denied prism (the original "zone exit"
  coincidence);
- 14 of 29 had mapped clearance below 0.5 m in the 3 s before onset, i.e.
  physical contact with an obstacle;
- the nearest obstacle was OB1 (the 11.5 m wall) in 17 of 29.
In the first logs examined, the vehicle's mapped clearance was 0.013 m, then
roll/pitch swung through +-70 degrees and vertical speed reached -20 m/s: a
collision followed by uncontrolled thrust. The "altitude spike" is the
vehicle being flung upward, and the logged GPS_RESTORED line coincided only
because the tumbling vehicle's position crossed out of the zone prism.

Expected behavior:
The mission completes without touching an obstacle or losing attitude control.

Actual behavior:
Roughly four in ten historical flights contained a loss of control.

Root causes (two distinct, both still open):
1. Blind sensor-only bypass flies the vehicle into obstacles: the bypass
   setpoint can sit centimetres from a wall because LiDAR sector ranges are
   smoothed (the wall looks farther away than it is while closing in) and the
   side sectors do not see a wall the vehicle is flying parallel to. Seen live
   in a velocity-PID flight: the vehicle slid along OB3's face at 0.07 m.
   Same family as BUG-008 and BUG-014.
2. The reactive sidestep can lock a target that does not actually clear the
   obstacle's corner (a straight line from the vehicle to a target 4 m
   sideways passes through OB1's east edge), and the impact can then eject
   the vehicle at tens of m/s (a simulator contact blow-up, seen at 22 m/s).

Fix:
Detection, not yet a cure:
- REQ-ATT-01 and REQ-CLEARANCE-02 in `vv_harness.py` flag every one of these
  flights; REQ-CLEARANCE-02 fires *before* the tumble (at 0.26 m with the
  vehicle still level), so it is an early warning, not just a post-mortem.
- The outer-loop velocity PID (`control_mode:=velocity_pid`) bounds commanded
  speed to 3 m/s / 1.5 m/s, which removes the position-setpoint step that
  produced violent tilts on its own. Measured so far on identical-start
  flights: position mode crashed to a 175 degree tilt; velocity-PID flights
  reached 86 degrees in one reactive-mission flight (contact with OB1) and
  44 / 31 / 28 / 27 degrees in the others. That is a small sample, not a
  statistical result -- it supports "gentler", not "fixed".
- On the A* course mission (route planned around the mapped obstacles) the
  velocity-PID flights completed to LANDING with 2.4 m minimum clearance.
The reactive avoidance geometry in root cause 1 and 2 is the unfixed part.

Regression test added:
`test_attitude_envelope_flags_a_tumble_before_the_failsafe` and
`test_airframe_contact_catches_a_graze_that_clearance_01_passes`
(`test_flight_vv.py`) encode the detectors against the shapes seen in the
logs. No regression test for the avoidance behavior itself yet.
