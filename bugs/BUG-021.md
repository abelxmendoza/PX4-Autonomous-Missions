BUG-021 (fixed -- N-drone reservation gridlock)

Scenario:
`SurveyCoordinator` generalized to N drones (`swarm_logic.Fleet`), run on the generated
layouts in `swarm_fleet_layout.py` with the seeded kinematic harness
(`test_swarm_invariants.run_scenario`). 30 scenarios per fleet size; every third has no
dropout, the rest lose one random drone at a random time. Kinematic model, not PX4.

Requirement violated:
Mission liveness: a mission that is physically possible should finish. Safety is NOT
violated: invariants #2, #3 and #5 held on every step of all 150 runs, and no two drones
came within the 2.5 m minimum separation (closest: 3.51 m).

Symptom (seed 0..29 per size):
| Drones | No dropout: COMPLETE | With a dropout: COMPLETE | Abort reason otherwise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10/10 | 0/20 | "no available vehicles" (by design: nobody to take over) |
| 2 | 10/10 | 14/20 | "mission timeout / blocked route" |
| 3 | 10/10 | 9/20 | "mission timeout / blocked route" |
| 5 | 10/10 | 8/20 | "mission timeout / blocked route" |
| 10 | 0/10 | 0/20 | "mission timeout / blocked route" |
With 10 drones, progress stops early (seed 0: 7 of 20 tasks done by 54.5 s) and nothing
moves until the 330 s timeout, so this is a deadlock, not a time budget.

Root cause (traced at t=150 s, 10 drones, seed 0; and 5 drones, seed 4):
Every waiting drone is holding because its next route segment passes within the 3.5 m
reservation of another drone, and that drone is itself holding: a cycle (px4_2 waits on
px4_9, which waits on px4_6; px4_1 waits on px4_5, which waits on px4_9; ...). Two
properties of the coordinator combine:
1. A* routes avoid obstacles, the GPS keep-out and *retired* (grounded) drones, but not
   drones that are hovering or holding, so a route can be planned straight past one.
2. The reservation check has no right-of-way: a blocked drone holds until the blocker
   moves, with no priority, yielding or replanning, so mutual blocking never resolves.
The only path north is an ~8 m corridor east of the GPS keep-out, which concentrates
traffic; the two-drone mission rarely meets the condition, ten drones always do.
Seed 10 of the two-drone harness (a grounded drone 1.8 m from a task point) is a
different, physical infeasibility and is characterized separately.

Fix:
Right-of-way alone does not cover a drone that has already landed: it cannot step
aside, and a route planned before it landed still clips its body. That case replans
around the grounded drone. Everything else is priority, a short reservation horizon,
and a sidestep.

1. Grants go out in a total order: a drone with a route before an idle hoverer, then
   lower vehicle number (`px4_1` before `px4_2` before `px4_10`). The loser holds.
   If it is sitting on the winner's next segment it sidesteps, toward its own route
   end when it has one, and that sidestep is kept until it arrives so it is not
   re-picked every tick. The sidestep is refused when it would enter the 3.5 m
   reservation or the 2.5 m floor.
2. The conflict check looks 5 m along the commanded leg, not the whole leg. The
   vehicle still flies the whole leg. Reserving a 30–40 m corridor leg was what
   made the drone behind wait until the leader had finished it.
3. After 8 s blocked by idle peers, the holder replans around those bodies only.
   Replanning around every holder every tick packed the corridor and made A*
   the bottleneck. A landed, disarmed peer is avoided at the 2.5 m floor rather
   than 3.5 m: the pads are 5.5 m apart, so two 3.5 m capsules overlap and there
   is no path between them. Airborne drones still keep 3.5 m. The predicted-
   separation check is unchanged.
4. A circular hold that lasts 15 s aborts with `deadlock: circular wait: ...`.
   A plain stall (nobody moving, no cycle) is reported as
   `no mission progress for 15s while drones hold` and left to the 330 s timeout,
   so two-vehicle seed 10 still ends as `mission timeout / blocked route`.

After (same 30 seeds, same 330 s timeout, same speeds):

| Drones | No dropout: COMPLETE | With a dropout: COMPLETE | Closest approach | Other endings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10/10 | 0/20 | n/a | "no available vehicles" (by design) |
| 2 | 10/10 | 16/20 | 3.31 m | 3 timeouts, 1 predicted-separation abort |
| 3 | 10/10 | 16/20 | 2.97 m | 2 timeouts, 2 predicted-separation aborts |
| 5 | 10/10 | 15/20 | 2.76 m | 4 timeouts, 1 reported circular wait |
| 10 | 10/10 | 11/20 | 2.51 m | 7 timeouts, 2 reported circular waits |

No run came inside 2.5 m. The 2.51 m figure is the 0.7 m/s ten-drone return,
which threads a 5.5 m pad gap at the safety floor. Three dropout runs (2-drone
seed 16, 3-drone seeds 11 and 16) now trip the existing 1 s predictor; sampled
separation on those runs stayed above 2.9 m. A grounded drone within 2.5 m of a
task point is still unreachable (two-vehicle seed 10; also px4_4 faulted at 80 s
on the ten-drone seed-0 layout, 2.49 m from land_4).

Regression tests:
`test_swarm_gridlock.py` (head-on pair, three-drone wait, the 10 no-dropout
ten-drone seeds, a dropout during a real hold, and seed 10 reported before the
timeout). `test_swarm_scaling.py` now requires the no-dropout ten-drone runs to
complete. The strict xfail is gone.
