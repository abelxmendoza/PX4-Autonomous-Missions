BUG-018

Scenario:
Live stereo + IMU fusion flights (`ekf_fusion_node`), course mission with fast yaw turns.

Requirement violated:
REQ-VO-DRIFT-01 (final drift 19.2% in flight H against 3.8% in flight G on the same mission).

Symptom:
The fusion heading drifted away from PX4's: 17 degrees of yaw error in flight H, constant after the turns, 2.5 degrees in flight G. Every later VO velocity was rotated into the wrong direction.

Root cause:
Attitude was pure gyro integration with no bias or scale correction. The error accumulated during fast yaw turns (about 10% of the rotation turned) and nothing ever pulled it back. VO's own rotation was not usable as a correction (BUG-017 follow-up evidence: it under-reads fast turns).

Fix:
`PoseVelocityEKF.apply_reference_attitude` slowly pulls the gyro attitude toward the autopilot's own attitude estimate (IMU + magnetometer AHRS, not ground truth), time constant `reference_attitude_tau_s` (default 2 s), applied from `VehicleAttitude` once VO has acquired. Position and velocity remain IMU + VO only.
Result: median yaw error 0 in flights K and L. Drift stayed at 9.3% (K) and 13.2% (L), so this removed a real fault but did not by itself bring drift under the 10% requirement; the remaining spread between K and L is unexplained.

Regression test added:
`test_reference_attitude_pulls_a_drifted_heading_back_without_stepping`, `test_reference_attitude_is_off_when_tau_is_zero` (`test_ekf_fusion.py`); `test_attitude_messages_pull_a_drifted_heading_toward_the_autopilot` (`test_ekf_fusion_node.py`).
